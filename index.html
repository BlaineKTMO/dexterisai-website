Projects

2026 · Venture studio, Los Angeles

Humanoid teleoperation and manipulation platform

An operator wearing a Meta Quest headset drives the robot's upper body directly, in real time. A web application gives people without a headset the same control from a browser. Obstacle avoidance and a whole-body control adaptor run underneath both, so neither operator has to think about the robot's limits.

Behind the teleoperation sits a reinforcement-learning training pipeline that produces manipulation policies and carries them across to the physical robot. Transfer is verified on hardware rather than reported from simulation. The client holds the source, the trained policies, and the pipeline, and their engineers run all three without us.
